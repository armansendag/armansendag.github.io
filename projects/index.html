Summer 2026 · No longer hosted

Bababooey Arena

A local and online card battler I made to experiment with game systems and learn from community feedback.

  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • WebSockets
  • PostgreSQL
  • Card collecting, pack opening, and deck building, with quests and rewards to support progression.
  • Friend challenges and casual and ranked matches, with moves checked by the server.

A technical challenge: keeping an online match consistent when a player disconnects or refreshes. The server manages the battle state and sends a fresh match snapshot when a player reconnects. Saved progress and match data can also be restored after a server restart.
